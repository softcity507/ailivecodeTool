Technical Overview

Understanding the logic behind our detection and humanization algorithms.

The Heuristics of Detection

Our detector uses a lightweight heuristic approach rather than a massive neural network. This allows for instant results directly in your browser without API latency.

1. Burstiness (Sentence Variance)

Variance = Σ(length - avgLength)² / N

Logic: AI models generate text token-by-token using probability distributions that favor "safe" and average sentence structures. This results in low standard deviation (low burstiness). Human writing is chaotic, mixing very short sentences with long, complex ones (high burstiness).

2. Perplexity (Simulated)

While we don't calculate true LLM perplexity locally, we approximate it via Lexical Diversity and Repetition Rate.

AI text often loops or repeats phrases when trying to meet word counts. We detect 3-gram repetitions and penalize the score accordingly.

Humanization Techniques

The Humanizer reverses these patterns:

  • Structural Injection: Deliberately breaks sentences to increase burstiness.
  • Stochastic Replacement: Swaps common words for contextually appropriate synonyms based on a randomized "temperature" (Level slider).
  • Imperfection Simulation: Optionally adds minor grammatical "flaws" or colloquialisms that strict LLMs avoid.
