Metrics Explained

  • Sentences: Total sentence count in the analyzed text.
  • Avg Length: Average number of words per sentence. AI tends to be very uniform (around 15-20 words).
  • Lexical Diversity: The ratio of unique words to total words. Humans typically use a wider vocabulary.
  • Burstiness: Measures the variation in sentence length. Humans write with high burstiness (mixing short and long sentences), while AI is consistent.

Usage Tips

For best results, input at least 30 words. The detector relies on statistical patterns which become more reliable with larger sample sizes.
