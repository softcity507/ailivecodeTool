AI Humanizer Guide

Transform robotic text into natural, human-sounding language.

Key Features

Tone & Style

Select from various tones such as Standard, Casual, Professional, Academic, or Creative to match your target audience.

Humanization Level

Adjust the intensity slider (1-100) to control how much the text is altered. Higher levels introduce more idioms, structural changes, and "human-like" imperfections.

How to Use

  1. Input Text: Paste your AI-generated text into the left panel.
  2. Configure: Choose your desired Tone and adjust the Humanization Level.
  3. Enhance: Check options like "Use Contractions" or "Insert Idioms" for better results.
  4. Process: Click "Humanize Text" and wait for the magic to happen.
  5. Output: Copy the result or save it as a text file.
