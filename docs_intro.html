Introduction

Welcome to the AI Tools Suite documentation.

About the Project

This suite provides advanced natural language processing tools designed to analyze and transform text. Whether you need to detect AI-generated content or humanize robotic text, our tools offer state-of-the-art solutions with an intuitive interface.

Available Tools

  • AI Humanizer: Rewrites AI-generated text to sound more natural and human-like using advanced synonym replacement and structural variance.
  • AI Content Detector: Analyzes text patterns to determine the likelihood of AI generation based on burstiness and perplexity metrics.
